FAITH, FLOWERS & THE GREEN MOUNTAIN STATE

A heart for people.
A love for the little things.

Ellen has called Vermont home since 2001, supporting her husband, Pastor Paul Lyon, in ministry. Her studies in psychology, social rehabilitation, and Christian ministry inform her writing.

Beyond the page, she enjoys music, art, sewing, gardening, and kayaking. She welcomes invitations to speak with ladies’ groups about her book.

Read Ellen’s full background +

Ellen is a graduate of Wesley Jr. College in Delaware, West Virginia Wesleyan (BA in Social Rehabilitation and Psychology), and the Wilson Apostolic School of Theology/Hope University (Master’s in Christian Ministry with a focus in Pastoral Care). She also received a certification in Soteric Counseling from Advanced Ministry (Jerry Ensey and Lynda Allison Doty). Since 1982, Ellen has faithfully served in her local United Pentecostal Church assemblies in varying capacities (Lexington Park, Maryland, and Burlington, Vermont). She served on the New Hampshire/Vermont United Pentecostal Church Ladies committee for many years. She has supported her husband, Pastor Paul Lyon, in ministry since moving to Vermont in 2001. Her hobbies include music, art, sewing, gardening, and kayaking. Ellen would consider it an honor to speak to your ladies’ group about this book.
